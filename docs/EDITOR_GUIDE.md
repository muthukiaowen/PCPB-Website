# PCPB website: guide for website staff

Sign in, then open **/page-builder** (or click **Edit this page** in the dark strip at the top of any page).

## New here?
Click **Getting started** in the website builder. It lists the set-up steps in order, and each step opens the right screen.

## Build and Live
The switch at the top of the builder has two positions.
- **Build**: you are only building. Links, buttons and forms on the page do nothing, so you cannot wander off the page by accident.
- **Live**: the tools disappear and the page behaves as a visitor sees it. Links open in a new tab, so your work stays open.

## Change a page
1. Open the page from the list and hover over a section. A small toolbar appears, with the section's name.
2. **Edit** opens the section's boxes on the right. Type, and the page changes as you type.
3. Pictures: drop a picture onto the picture box, or click it to choose one.
4. **Move** drags the section; the arrows move it one step; **Page order** on the left reorders a long page easily. **Duplicate** copies the section below itself. **Hide** keeps it but hides it from the public. **Tint** gives it a shaded background.
5. Add a section: drag it from the list on the left onto the page, or click it to add it at the end.
6. **Save and send for approval** keeps your changes and passes them to the approver in one click. Visitors still see the old version until it is published. **Save draft** keeps your changes without sending them, if you are not finished.
7. Approvers and publishers see their own buttons (**Approve**, **Publish**, **Send Back**). Which buttons you see depends on your role.

## Carousel
Cards, Leadership and News sections can be shown as a carousel: **Edit** the section and set **Layout** to **Carousel**. Visitors move through it with the arrows or by swiping. Tick **Carousel moves by itself** to have it advance without the visitor.

## Translation
The website is written once, in English. Visitors use the **Translate** button at the top of every page to read it in another language (Google Translate does the translation). You do not type translations anywhere. Register entries such as product names and registration numbers are never translated.

## New page
**+ New page**, give it a title, choose a starting layout, and tick **Add it to the main menu** if needed.

## Things that update by themselves
You do not edit these on the page. Add or change the record and every page that shows it updates.

| To change | Go to |
|---|---|
| News, notices, circulars, safety alerts, the ticker | Desk > News and notices |
| Tenders | Desk > Tenders (a tender past its closing time moves to the archive) |
| Vacancies | HR publishes a Job Opening; it appears on Careers |
| Board and management | Desk > Leadership |
| Forms, guidelines, reports | Desk > Documents |
| Registers | Desk > Registers |
| How a service is described | Desk > Service descriptions |
| Address, phone, map, offices, fixed wording, languages offered by Translate | Desk > Settings and theme |

## Managers only
- **Logo and name**: upload the logo, change the organisation name, the footer logo and the browser tab icon.
- **Theme**: colours, fonts, corner roundness, page width. Applies to the whole site at once.
- **Menu, footer and addresses**: drag to reorder; put an item under another to make a drop-down; add short addresses such as `jobs` going to `/careers`.
- **Page settings**: change a page's address (the old one forwards) or make it the home page.

## A new register in four steps
Desk > Registers > New. Give it a title, choose the ERP record type it reads, say which records are public, press **List all fields of the record type**, and tick the fields the public may see. Publish. It appears on the Registers page, in the search and in the product status check.
